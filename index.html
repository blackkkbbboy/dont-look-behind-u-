The rules

  • Something is in the building with you.
  • It only moves when you are not looking at it. Keep it in your view and it freezes.
  • It gets smarter, faster and bolder the longer you survive.
  • It hates light, but lights fail. Spare batteries are rare.
  • Lamp-lit rooms are safe. Usually.
  • Read every note. Something happened here.
  • There are three endings.
